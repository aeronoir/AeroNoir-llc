How to Use the Deck

A system, not a cram session.

1
Filter by domain
Start with your weakest area — not alphabetically, not randomly. If sectional charts feel foreign, start there. If regulations feel solid, save those for the end.
2
Read the question, commit to an answer
Say your answer out loud or write it down before flipping. The act of retrieving — not just reading — is what builds retention.
3
Mark Know It or Review
Sort as you go. Green means it's locked in. Orange means come back. By the end of each session you'll see exactly where your gaps are.
4
Drill the Review cards daily
Run through only your flagged cards the next day. Repeat until the orange list is empty. That's when you're ready to sit for the exam.
